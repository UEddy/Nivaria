Frequently asked questions

What is Nivaria?

An AI agent for consumer brands. Name your brand and one or two rivals, and it reads Qloo taste data on both audiences and writes a brief: the segments the rival owns, where they are in your city, and which to win first.

Where does the data come from?

Qloo's Taste AI, which links tastes across music, film, TV, places and brands. Every score in a brief is a Qloo number attached by the server, and a claim the model cannot back with a Qloo result is dropped.

What does the AI model see?

Only the brand, rival and city you type, and Qloo's results. Never your account details. Audiences are described by their tastes, places, age and gender, never by protected traits.

Do I need a city?

No. Without one you get the audience gaps, demographics and positioning. With one you also get places and the map.

How long does a brief take?

A few minutes at most. You watch each step live, and the finished brief is saved to your runs so you can open it again or run it with fresh data.

What is the live demo?

A shared account with finished example briefs, so you can look around without signing up. Briefs you run there are visible to other demo visitors.

What does it cost?

Nivaria is free to use.
