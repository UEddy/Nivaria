Taste Gap

Your rival's customers are not a mystery. Their tastes are measurable.

Share of voice tells you who is louder. Taste Gap tells you who your rival reaches that you do not, what those people love, and where they are.

Find the segments they own

The artists, shows, films and brands your rival's audience over-indexes on and yours does not, grouped into segments you can name and target.

See where they are on the map

Add a city and the agent compares both audiences neighborhood by neighborhood, so you know where the rival leads and where you already win.

Know which to win first

A ranked list with the reason, a campaign hook drawn from the actual tastes, and concrete channels, like a creator partnership or a pop-up in a named neighborhood.

The problem

Your rival's fans binge a reality show you have never heard of and pack a neighborhood you have never run a campaign in.

Surveys are slow, social listening is noisy, and demographics alone say little about what people actually love. So brands keep fighting over the same audience while the rival quietly owns the next one.

How it works

From two brand names to a ranked plan in a few minutes

An AI agent does the research. You watch every step it takes, live.

1

Name your brand and rivals

Type your brand, one or two rivals and, if you like, a city. Suggestions come straight from Qloo, so every name resolves to a real brand.

2

The agent plans the research

It decides which comparisons to run and fires them in parallel: audience gaps per domain, demographics, brand positioning and the city map.

3

Qloo answers with taste data

Each comparison scores both audiences on the same artists, shows, films, places and brands, so every gap is a like-for-like number.

4

You get a cited brief

Segments the rival owns, where they are on the map, who they are, and what to win first. Every claim links back to the Qloo score behind it.

Powered by Qloo

Built on Qloo's Taste AI

Qloo maps how tastes connect across music, film, TV, dining, places and brands. Taste Gap turns that into a head-to-head read on two audiences. Without Qloo there is no brief: every number in it comes from a Qloo response.

Audience gaps

Qloo Insights lists the rival audience's top artists, films, shows, places and brands, then scores your audience on exactly those entities. The difference is the gap.

The geo-gap map

Qloo heatmaps for both brands in one city land on the same grid. Taste Gap joins them cell by cell and labels the hotspots with real neighborhoods.

Who the audiences are

Qloo demographics show how each audience over or under-indexes by age and gender, side by side.

Brand positioning

Qloo's compare analysis shows the tags both brands share and the ones only the rival carries.

Brand search

Qloo search resolves what you type to the right brand and city, and powers the suggestions in the form.

Numbers the model cannot invent

The agent cites Qloo rows by reference and the server attaches the scores. A citation that does not match a Qloo result is dropped.

What is in a Taste Gap brief

  • Rival-owned segments Clusters of artists, shows, films, places and brands where the rival's audience over-indexes and yours does not.
  • Win-first ranking The segments to go after first, each with the reason, a campaign hook and concrete channels.
  • Geo-gap map Both audiences compared across a city, with the rival's strongest neighborhoods and yours.
  • Demographics Age and gender mix of each audience on one chart, with a table view.
  • Brand positioning The tags both brands share and the ones only the rival carries.
  • Cited evidence Every claim carries the Qloo scores behind it, attached by the server, never typed by the model.
  • Live agent trace Each step the agent takes, streamed as it happens and kept with the brief.
  • Two rivals at once Compare against one or two rivals, with the map and charts switchable per rival.
  • Runs history Every brief you have run, ready to reopen or run again with fresh data.

Taste Gap works on aggregated, anonymized taste data from Qloo. Only the brand, rival and city you type are sent to the AI model, never your account details. Audiences are described by their tastes, places, age and gender, never by protected traits.
