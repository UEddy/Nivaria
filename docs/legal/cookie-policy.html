Cookie Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage that Nivaria ("Nivaria," "we," "us" or "our") uses on nivaria.app and in the Nivaria app. It is short because we use very little: one first-party cookie to keep you signed in, and a few settings saved in your own browser. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. A first-party cookie is set by the site you are visiting. A third-party cookie is set by another company, often for advertising or analytics. Browsers also offer "local storage," which lets a site remember small settings on your device without sending them to the server.

Settings saved in your browser

These are stored in your browser's local storage or session storage. They never leave your device and are not used to track you.

Other services our pages load

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and brief maps load map tiles from OpenStreetMap. These requests send your IP address and browser details to those services so they can deliver the files. Nivaria does not use them to set cookies or track you. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, web beacons or fingerprinting, and we do not show ads. Because we only use a strictly necessary cookie, we do not show a cookie consent banner. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the cs.sid cookie you will not be able to sign in, but the public pages and shared brief links still work. Clearing your browser's site data removes the saved settings above.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the cookies we use change, and change the date at the top. Questions: support@nivaria.app.
