Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Nivaria ("Nivaria," "we," "us" or "our") collects when you use the website at nivaria.app and the Nivaria app (together, the "Services"), how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. If you have a question, email support@nivaria.app.

What Nivaria is

Nivaria is audience intelligence for consumer brands. You name a brand and, optionally, one or two rivals and a city. An AI agent researches both audiences with Qloo's Taste AI and writes a brief: the taste segments the rival's audience owns, where on the city map the rival leads, who each audience is by age and gender, and which segments to win first. Every number in a brief comes from Qloo.

Nivaria works with brand names and city names. It does not need, ask for or analyze information about individual consumers. The audience data in a brief is Qloo's aggregated, anonymized taste data, not data about any person.

Summary

  • We collect only what an account needs (your first name, email address, password and timezone), the briefs you run, the Slack or Discord webhooks you choose to save, and limited technical data to keep the Services secure and fair.
  • When you run a brief, the brand, rival and city you typed are sent to Qloo and to an AI model provider. Your name, email address and other account details are never sent to either.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • You can export your data or delete your account at any time from Profile.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. To sign up you give your first name, email address and a password, and your browser's timezone is recorded. Your password is stored only as a bcrypt hash. We email you a one-time code to confirm your address, and record when you accepted our Terms of Service, this Privacy Policy and our Cookie Policy, and which versions.
  • Google sign-in. Where Sign in with Google is offered and you use it, Google shares your name, email address and Google account identifier with us. We use them only to create and sign in to your account.
  • Briefs. For each brief we store the brand, rivals and city you typed, the finished brief, the Qloo data behind it, the agent's trace (each step it took), which AI model wrote it and how long it took.
  • Webhooks. If you save a Slack or Discord webhook URL so briefs can be sent to your team, we store it with your account settings.
  • Share links. If you share a brief, we store the link's random token, which brief it opens and when it was made or revoked.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we keep the message and our reply.

Information collected automatically

  • Session cookie. A first-party cookie keeps you signed in. See our Cookie Policy.
  • IP addresses, hashed. To limit how many fresh briefs each visitor can run (three a day by default, one at a time) and to keep a security audit log (sign-ins, password changes, data exports, account deletions), we store a salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address, never the address itself. The audit log also keeps the first 100 characters of your browser's user agent.
  • Server logs. Our server logs record the request method, path, status and an IP address with its last part masked. They never record request bodies, passwords or headers.
  • Sign-in attempts. We count failed sign-in attempts per email address to block password guessing.

We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and confirm your email address.
  • To run the briefs you ask for, store them, show them to you and send them where you choose.
  • To keep the Services fair and available: per-visitor limits on fresh briefs, rate limits and a 14-day cache that answers a repeated brand, rival and city from a recent brief.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate security issues and comply with the law.
  • To answer your messages. We send email only for account purposes (confirmation codes, password resets and account notices), never marketing.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on performance of our contract with you (running the Services you asked for), our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention and fair use) and legal obligations.

3. Qloo and AI model providers

Running a brief sends the brand, rival and city names you typed to two kinds of service:

  • Qloo (Qloo Taste AI) receives the brand, rival and city names, and the text you type into the brand field for autocomplete suggestions. Qloo returns aggregated taste data about the audiences of those brands.
  • An AI model provider receives the brand, rival and city names together with the Qloo results, and writes the brief. Nivaria tries providers in order and moves to the next one if a provider is busy or unavailable. By default these are Google (Gemini models), Groq and OpenRouter (which routes requests to the host of the chosen model). Z.ai, Anthropic, Baseten, Cerebras and GitHub Models can be enabled by the operator. The brief shows which model wrote it.

Your name, email address, password, webhooks and other account details are never sent to Qloo or to a model provider. The brand, rival and city fields are capped in length, and anything in them shaped like an email address or phone number is removed before a model sees it. Some model providers offer free tiers whose terms let them keep prompts or use them to improve their services, so please type only brand and city names, never confidential or personal information.

Briefs are written by AI from Qloo data. The model cites Qloo results by reference, and our server attaches Qloo's own numbers and removes claims it cannot match to Qloo data, but a brief can still contain mistakes. See our Terms of Service.

4. The shared demo account

The Try the live demo button signs you into one account shared by every demo visitor. Briefs run in the demo account can be seen by other demo visitors, and the app says so in a banner. The demo account cannot be renamed, deleted or have its password changed. A webhook you paste while using it is kept only in your own browser session, not with the shared account. Please do not enter personal information in the demo account.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Hosting. The Services and their database run on a cloud server we rent from DigitalOcean.
  • Email. Resend sends our confirmation, password reset and account emails, so it receives your email address and the message.
  • Qloo and AI model providers, as described in section 3. They receive brand, rival and city names and Qloo results, not your account details.
  • Slack and Discord, only when you send a brief there. The message contains the brief's headline, its top picks and a share link.
  • Google, if you use Sign in with Google. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • OpenStreetMap. Brief maps load map tiles from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which receive your IP address and browser details.
  • People you share a brief with. A share link opens a read-only copy of the brief for anyone who has the link. It shows the brief only, not your name or email address. Share pages tell search engines not to index them, and you can revoke a link at any time.
  • Legal and safety. When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or Nivaria.
  • Business transfers. If Nivaria is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of it, under this policy's protections.

6. How long we keep information

  • Your account, briefs, settings, webhooks and share links are kept until you delete your account or we close it.
  • Deleting your account removes them immediately and permanently, along with your confirmation codes and sign-in attempt records. Share links you made stop working.
  • Security audit log entries are kept after deletion so the security record stays complete. They identify the deleted account only by an internal number and a salted hash of its email address.
  • Confirmation codes expire shortly after they are sent. A sign-in session ends when you close your browser, or after 30 days if you choose "Remember me."

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability. In Profile, Export my data downloads a JSON file with your account details, settings, briefs, share links and your security audit log entries.
  • Correction. You can change your name and password, and change or remove your webhooks, in Profile.
  • Deletion. In Profile you can delete your account. You confirm with your password and by typing your email address, and the deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Share links and webhooks. You can revoke a share link or remove a webhook at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada or a US state with a consumer privacy law), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. Email support@nivaria.app to exercise any right. We will not treat you differently for exercising it.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, IP addresses are stored only as salted hashes, every state-changing request is checked against a CSRF token, and sign-in, signup and brief requests are rate limited. Deleting an account requires your password. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of a breach that affects it as the law requires.

9. International transfers

Nivaria is based in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, for transfers out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland.

10. Children

The Services are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the Services or our practices change, and change the date at the top. If a change is material, we will tell signed-in users in the app or by email before it takes effect.
